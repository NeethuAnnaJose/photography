Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our photography sessions and booking process.

How do I book a session? +

Booking is easy! Fill out the contact form or email me directly with your preferred dates and session type. I'll respond within 24 hours with availability and a detailed quote. A 30% deposit secures your date.

How long until I receive my photos? +

Turnaround time is typically 2-3 weeks for edited galleries. Rush delivery within 5 business days is available for an additional fee. You'll receive a private online gallery with high-resolution downloads.

What is your editing style? +

My editing style is clean, natural, and timeless with a focus on true-to-life colors and beautiful light. I avoid heavy filters and trendy edits that may not age well, ensuring your photos look stunning for years to come.

Do you travel for shoots? +

Yes! I love traveling for destination shoots and am available worldwide. Travel fees vary based on location and are discussed during the booking process. I frequently shoot across the country and internationally.

Can I order prints? +

Absolutely! I partner with professional fine art print labs to offer museum-quality prints, canvases, and albums. Print ordering is available through your private gallery, and I'm happy to help with size and finish recommendations.
